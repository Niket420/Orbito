What it does

Talk to it the way you'd talk to a person.

No commands to learn. Orbito works out what you mean and picks the right steps. Here's some of what you can say.

How it works

From your voice to your Mac in about three seconds.

  1. 01

    Listen

    Tap ⌃⌥ and talk. A tiny on-device model notices when you stop talking. Audio is never saved.

    on your Mac
  2. 02

    Understand

    Whisper turns speech into text, using your installed app names so "Chrome" isn't heard as "crown".

    ~0.4 s
  3. 03

    Plan

    An LLM turns your words into a short list of allowed steps. It can't invent new actions or run shell commands.

    ~1.5 s
  4. 04

    Check

    Every step is validated twice, by the backend and again by the Mac app. Risky ones wait for your "yes".

    instant
  5. 05

    Do

    Orbito runs each step through macOS itself and confirms it worked before moving on.

    on your Mac

Safety

Powerful, but it asks first.

An assistant that can click and type for you needs guardrails. These are built in, not optional.

Approval for anything risky

Typing, key presses, force quit, moving files to the Trash, locking the Mac, and buttons named Delete, Buy or Send all wait for you. Say "yes" or "no", or click.

A fixed list of actions

The AI picks from 30 allowed actions. Anything else is rejected, by the backend and again by the app, even if the AI gets it wrong.

Things it will never do

Run shell commands, enter passwords or payment details, send messages or email, install software, empty the Trash, restart or shut down.

Private by default

Audio is never saved. Logs record what kind of step ran, never what you said or typed. Screen text is only read when you ask about your screen.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

When can I try it?

Orbito is in private testing now. Join the waitlist and you'll get an invite as soon as early access opens.

Is Orbito free?

Yes, it's free. The code will be open source once Orbito is ready for everyone. Join the waitlist and you'll hear the moment it's out.

Which Macs does it run on?

Macs with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) running macOS 14 Sonoma or later. The notch panel works best on MacBooks with a notch, but Orbito works on any display.

Does it work offline?

Noticing when you stop talking happens on your Mac. Understanding and planning use cloud models, so you need an internet connection.

Is my voice recorded?

No recording is ever saved. Audio is turned into text and then discarded. Logs keep only what type of step ran, never your words.

Can it do things I didn't ask for?

No. It can only use a fixed list of actions, each step is checked twice, and risky steps wait for your approval. It can't run shell commands or send messages.

Report a bug

Something broke? Tell us.

Already testing Orbito? This opens a pre-filled issue on GitHub, where you can add screenshots before you submit. Nothing is sent until you do.

Helpful extra: Orbito's log is at ~/Library/Application Support/Orbito/backend.log (or Settings → Show log). It never contains what you said.
